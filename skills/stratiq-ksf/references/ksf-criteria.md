# KSF tests, sources, and scoring rules

Read this during steps 2 and 5 of the ksfs workflow.

## Three tests (a candidate must pass all three)

1. **Differentiating:** Leaders and laggards visibly differ on it. If every player scores 7–8, it's table stakes, not a KSF. Move it to a "threshold requirements" note.
2. **Measurable:** At least one indicator can be observed from disclosures, data, or credible third-party evidence.
3. **Controllable:** A firm can build it through its own decisions. Industry conditions like "a favorable regulatory climate" are PESTEL factors, not KSFs.

## Where candidates come from

| Toolkit input | Question to ask | Example KSF |
|---|---|---|
| Porter's Five Forces: strong buyer power | What lowers buyers' leverage? | Switching-cost-creating integration |
| Porter's Five Forces: supplier power | What reduces dependence on suppliers? | Procurement scale or vertical integration |
| Porter's Five Forces: threat of substitutes | What keeps value above substitutes? | Outcome-proven differentiation |
| Porter's Five Forces: rivalry | What wins share without a price war? | Brand/channel access, cost leadership |
| Porter's Five Forces: new entrants | What protects incumbents? | Regulatory expertise, network effects |
| Trending Influence Factors | What capability will this shift require? | Digital/AI operations capability |
| PESTEL | Which external shift rewards which capability? | Compliance agility, talent access |
| Competitive analysis | What do the leaders have that laggards lack? | Whatever cross-peer insight points to |

## Anchor scale (adapt the wording for each KSF)

- **9–10:** Clear industry leader. The indicator is in the top quartile, and the advantage is hard to copy.
- **6–8:** Above median or improving. The advantage is real but can be matched.
- **4–5:** Around the median. No meaningful advantage.
- **2–3:** Bottom quartile or deteriorating.
- **1:** A material weakness that is visibly costing share or margin.

## Missing evidence

- If a competitor is missing evidence for up to 1 KSF, score it `n/a`. The script rescales that competitor's weights over the KSFs it does have and flags it.
- If a competitor is missing evidence for more than 1 KSF, list it as "insufficient evidence" and leave it out of the ranking.
- For private competitors, proxy evidence (pricing, headcount, customer reviews, funding) is allowed. Mark it confidence = Low.
