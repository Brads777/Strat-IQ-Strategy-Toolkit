# GLO-BUS CIR gap analysis: student fill-in template

Copy the block below into a new chat in your StratOS Project, replace every bracket with your figures,
and send. It takes 3-5 minutes. Leave a line as `n/a` if your report doesn't show it; never guess.

Where the figures come from:
- **Competitive Intelligence Report (CIR):** each company's price, P/Q rating, models, advertising,
  retailer support or promotions, warranty and market share, by product and region.
- **Camera & Drone Journal (CDJ):** the scoreboard, and the benchmarking pages with low, average and
  high cost per unit and operating margin.

## What you get back

1. **Strategic group maps** for cameras and drones (price against P/Q, bubble = share), with every
   company placed as low-cost, premium differentiator, value leader or stuck in the middle.
2. **The share leader and its driver** (price cut, advertising, retailer support, models or P/Q), and
   whether that share was bought with margin.
3. **The weakest rival** and why it is likely to lose share next year.
4. **White space** you can actually reach, and space you can't.
5. **Hold, pivot or move** for each product line.
6. **3-5 moves** across the five decision screens, each with its effect on EPS, ROE, stock price,
   credit rating and image rating, checked against the guardrails.

**You still decide and enter every number.** Test each move in the simulator's projections and keep
it only if the overall projection improves.

## Using the real industries too

Before your first round, or whenever a lever doesn't make sense, ask: *"Run the GLO-BUS Coach,
mode 1, on the drone industry."* StratOS benchmarks real firms (DJI, Skydio, Autel, Parrot, Yuneec
for drones; GoPro, Garmin, DJI Osmo, Insta360, Akaso for cameras) and explains which GLO-BUS lever
each finding maps to. Real companies are analogues, not answer keys: never copy their numbers into
the game.
