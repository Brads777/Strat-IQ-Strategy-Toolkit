StratOS Parts 2 + 3: install and run

One upload adds the internal analysis (Part 2), the tools that make the strategy work (Part 3), and new GLO-BUS help with the real camera and drone industries. Release v3.0.0.

The short version. Download stratos-part2-3-skills.zip, upload the 27 skill zips inside it, switch off the old Part 1 orchestrator, replace industries.json in your Project, and type "Run StratOS". StratOS reads your saved ledger and carries on from the last step you finished.

Install on Claude.ai (about 15 minutes)

  1. Check code execution is on. Settings → Capabilities → Code execution and file creation. If Part 1 works for you, it already is.
  2. Download the upload. Open the latest StratOS release (v3.0.0) and, under Assets, download stratos-part2-3-skills.zip. Unzip it once. Inside are 27 smaller zips, one per skill. Do not unzip those.
  3. Delete the copies being replaced. In the Skills list, delete stratos-case-exhibits, stratos-strategic-mapping and stratos-globus-coach. If you installed the earlier Part 2 (v1.7.0), also delete stratos-orchestrator-final, stratos-value-chain, stratos-resources-capabilities, stratos-vrio and stratos-swot.
  4. Upload all 27 zips. Skills → Upload skill, once per zip.
  5. Turn off the old orchestrator. Switch off stratos-orchestrator. Leave stratos-orchestrator-final on.
    Do not skip this. With both on, "Run StratOS" can open the old Part 1 version, which knows nothing about Parts 2 and 3.
  6. Check the count. You should see 37 StratOS skills switched on. If your account limits enabled skills, keep the orchestrator and this week's steps on; the others still run from the standard framework, with a note saying so.
  7. Update your Project knowledge. Replace industries.json with the new version (it adds cameras and drones). Keep your latest strategy-ledger file there too, and only one per industry.

Check it works

  1. Start a new chat inside the Project and type: Run StratOS The intro should show three parts and modes A to E.
  2. Read what it found. Before running anything it reports what your ledger holds, for example "Parts 1 and 2 complete for the global EV industry, base company BYD, as of 2026-10-01."

GLO-BUS: get unstuck

Three ways the coach helps. Your team still makes and enters every decision.

  1. Learn the real industry behind the game. Run the GLO-BUS Coach, mode 1, on the drone industry. StratOS benchmarks real firms (DJI, Skydio, Autel, Parrot, Yuneec; for cameras GoPro, Garmin, DJI Osmo, Insta360, Akaso) and explains which GLO-BUS lever each finding maps to: why retailer discounts eat margin, why R&D scale lowers cost per unit, how low-cost and premium players differ. Real companies are analogues, not answer keys. Never copy their numbers into the game.
  2. After every round: the CIR gap analysis. Open your Competitive Intelligence Report and the Camera & Drone Journal. Type Run the GLO-BUS Coach, mode 2. Give me the fill-in template. Paste each company's price, P/Q rating, models, advertising, retailer support, warranty and market share (3 to 5 minutes). You get strategic group maps for cameras and drones, who won share and whether it was profitable, the weakest rival, the white space you can actually reach, and 3 to 5 moves for next year checked against the guardrails.
  3. Review the whole year automatically (optional). With the Claude in Chrome extension, open GLO-BUS in Chrome and sign in yourself, then type Capture our GLO-BUS year and review it. Claude walks the menus read-only, saves your decision screens and the class reports into one capture file, and the coach comments on your inputs against what happened. It never sees your password and never changes or submits a decision. No extension? Save the screens as screenshots or PDFs and upload them instead.
  4. Before entering decisions: year coaching. Coach our Year 7 decisions. Our strategy is best-cost in cameras and differentiation in drones. It diagnoses last year against the five scored KPIs (EPS, ROE, stock price, credit rating, image rating) and proposes a few small moves. Test each one in the simulator's projections and keep it only if the overall projection improves.
